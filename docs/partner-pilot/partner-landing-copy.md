## Page Copy Structure

### [Above the Fold — Hero]

**H1:**
> When staying at home is getting harder, understanding your options shouldn't be.

**Subtitle paragraph:**
> {{PARTNER}} helps families support independent living. When a family starts asking what comes next, Assistedly gives them an independent way to understand care options and decide whether to explore a higher level of care.

**Trust badge:**
> [Shield icon] Assistedly does not rank or select facilities on behalf of {{PARTNER}}. Our recommendations are independent.

**CTA button:**
> Start your free care snapshot

**Secondary text (under CTA):**
> No pressure. No sales calls. Takes 3 minutes.

---

### [Section: What to Expect — 3 Steps]

**H2:** What to expect

**Step 1 — A short, independent assessment**
> Answer 5–8 questions about current living situation, care needs, and priorities. No medical diagnosis required.

**Step 2 — Your personalized care snapshot**
> Receive a transparent summary with source-linked explanations. See where the information comes from and why it matters.

**Step 3 — You decide what's next**
> Browse care options on your own, save your snapshot, or ask Assistedly to help connect you. Your choice, always.

---

### [Section: Independence Promise]

**H2:** Our independence promise

| Promise | Detail |
|---|---|
| {{PARTNER}} does not choose or rank your care options | The assessment is independent. Recommendations are based on state-verified data, not partner influence. |
| We do not sell your personal information | Your contact information stays with Assistedly unless you separately authorize sharing. |
| Transparent economics | Any fee Assistedly earns comes from participating communities only if you choose to move in. |
| You control the process | Stop at any time. Save your snapshot. Browse without commitment. |

---

### [Section: How It Works (Visual/Accordion)]

**H2:** How this works

**How does the partnership between {{PARTNER}} and Assistedly work?**
> {{PARTNER}} offers families a co-branded resource when care needs may be changing. The assessment and navigation experience is provided entirely by Assistedly. {{PARTNER}} does not influence which facilities appear, how they are ranked, or what recommendations are made.

**Will {{PARTNER}} know my personal information?**
> Only if you separately authorize it. By default, {{PARTNER}} receives only aggregate information: how many families clicked the link, how many completed the assessment, and whether families found it useful.

**Is this a referral or placement service?**
> No. Assistedly is a care-navigation and information service. We help families understand their options with transparent, state-verified data. Any connection to a specific facility happens only if you choose it.

**What does it cost?**
> Nothing for families. If you choose to move into a participating community, that community may pay Assistedly a fee. This is disclosed at every relevant point.
