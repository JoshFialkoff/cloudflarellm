# Partner Outreach Email Sequence

> 3-email sequence for first-contact partner outreach
> Subject lines A/B test recommended
> All emails: no images (deliverability), mobile-friendly, one clear CTA

---

## Email 1 — Introduction

**Subject options (A/B test):**
- A: "A next step for {{PARTNER}} families when care needs change"
- B: "Partnership idea: helping {{PARTNER}} families navigate what comes next"

**Preview text:** Independent care-navigation for families whose needs are evolving beyond home safety.

---

Hi [First Name],

{{PARTNER}} helps families support independent living. That's an important job — and families love you for it.

I'm reaching out because we've seen something across the senior-safety industry: when a family's needs evolve beyond what home safety alone can address, most companies have nothing to offer.

Families search alone, find commission-driven referral sites, or wait until a crisis forces a rushed decision.

**That's where Assistedly comes in.**

We're building the independent care-transition layer for companies that help older adults remain at home. When a family starts asking "what comes next," {{PARTNER}} can offer them:

- An independent, state-verified assessment of care options
- Transparent data on Massachusetts facilities (staffing, inspections, costs)
- A personalized care snapshot the family controls

{{PARTNER}} doesn't choose or rank the facilities. We handle the navigation experience. You give customers a useful next step and receive measurable insight into the transition journey.

**What we'd propose:** A 90-day pilot. One partner, one channel, clear metrics. Minimal integration — just a co-branded link in your existing customer communication.

If this resonates, I'd love to share our pilot brief and answer any questions.

Would you be open to a 15-minute call this week or next?

Be well,

Josh Fialkoff
Founder, Assistedly.ai
josh@assistedly.ai
https://assistedly.ai/partners

P.S. — We're not a referral agency. We don't sell lead lists. Our revenue comes from participating communities only if a family chooses to move in. Full disclosure, always.

## Email 2 — Social Proof + Education

**Send:** 5–7 days after Email 1 (if no reply)

**Subject options:**
- A: "What 90 days taught us about care transitions"
- B: "Why families don't trust senior-living referrals (and what we're doing about it)"

---

Hi [First Name],

Quick follow-up on my note about an Assistedly partnership.

I wanted to share something we've learned from talking to families:

**The #1 reason families delay exploring care options is distrust.**

They've been burned by "free" referral services that sell their contact info to 5+ communities. They don't know who pays for the service. They can't tell if the recommendations are unbiased.

That's why we built Assistedly differently:

- **Source-linked everything.** Every recommendation shows state licensing data, inspection history, and staffing ratios. Families can verify for themselves.
- **No selling contact info.** We don't buy or sell lead lists. Period.
- **Transparent economics.** We disclose who pays and how, at the point of every recommendation.

For partners like {{PARTNER}}, this means you can offer families a resource they'll actually trust — not another pitch.

I've attached a short pilot brief (1 page) that outlines how a 90-day pilot would work. If you'd like to discuss, I'm happy to work around your schedule.

Be well,

Josh Fialkoff
Founder, Assistedly.ai
josh@assistedly.ai

**Subject options:**

If this isn't the right time, I totally understand. If it is, I'd love to walk through the pilot brief together.

## Follow-Up Strategy (If No Reply After 3 Emails)

1. **LinkedIn connection request** with a short note: "Reached out about a care-transition partnership — would love to connect."
2. **Phone call** to main line (if available) — ask for partnerships or business development
3. **Warm introduction** — check for mutual LinkedIn connections
4. **Add to nurture list** — check back in 60–90 days with industry insights or new pilot results

## A/B Test Recommendations

| Element | Option A | Option B |
|---|---|---|
| Subject line (Email 1) | "A next step for..." | "Partnership idea:..." |
| Opening hook | Problem-first (families delay) | Value-first (better support) |
| CTA | "Would you be open to a 15-minute call?" | "Can I send you our pilot brief?" |
| P.S. placement | Disclosure/trust signal | Social proof stat |

Track: open rate, reply rate, call-booking rate per variant.
