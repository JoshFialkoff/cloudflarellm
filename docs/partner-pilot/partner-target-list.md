## Scoring Methodology

Each company scored 0–5 on:
- **Channel fit** (existing customer touchpoint for insertion)
- **Volume** (enough customers for 90-day pilot signal)
- **Transition gap** (no competing care-transition product)
- **Trust alignment** (brand tone aligns with empathy + independence)
- **MA readiness** (can serve MA families in pilot)

**Max score: 25**

| 21–25 | Interview Priority |

## Interview Operator Questions (Recommended)

For each Interview Priority company, ask:

1. How do you currently handle conversations when a customer or family member asks about care options beyond home safety?
2. What customer communication channels do you use for annual reviews, wellness checks, or retention outreach?
3. Have you explored partnerships in the senior-living or care-navigation space? What happened?
4. What metrics matter most to you in a partner pilot (retention, satisfaction, revenue, engagement)?
5. How do you protect customer trust when introducing third-party services?
6. What is your current process for identifying customers whose needs may be evolving beyond your product?
7. What legal or compliance review does a new partner program typically require?
