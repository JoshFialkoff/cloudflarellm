# Platform Terms of Service Compliance Analysis
## For Pat/Maggie Organic Outreach Strategy

**Date:** August 10, 2026
**Scope:** Reddit, Quora, AgingCare.com, Facebook, Nextdoor
**Risk Level:** 🟡 MODERATE-HIGH — Requires significant amendments to current playbook

---

## Executive Summary

After reviewing the Terms of Service for all five target platforms, **the current Pat/Maggie playbook requires substantial amendments to avoid account bans and legal exposure.** No platform explicitly permits the two-layer AI/human outreach model as currently designed. Key risks include:

1. **Reddit**: Strict 90/10 self-promotion rule; bot/automation prohibitions; impersonation risks
2. **Quora**: No affiliate links; spam policy prohibits "promoting products not relevant"; AI moderation is aggressive
3. **AgingCare.com**: Owned by A Place For Mom (competitor); explicitly prohibits commercial use and scraping
4. **Facebook**: Fake account policy; spam detection; inauthentic behavior prohibitions
5. **Nextdoor**: Commercial posts from personal accounts prohibited; business account required for promotion

**CRITICAL FINDING:** AgingCare.com is owned by A Place For Mom (APFM), Assistedly.ai's primary competitor. Any activity there benefits a competitor and violates their ToS commercial use clause.

## 1. REDDIT — Risk Level: 🟡 MODERATE

### Relevant Policies
- **User Agreement Section 7**: Prohibits using Services "in any manner (automated, including via bots, or otherwise) that could interfere with, disable, disrupt, overburden, or otherwise impair the Services"
- **User Agreement Section 7**: Prohibits "accessing, searching, or collecting data from the Services by any means (automated or otherwise) except as permitted... scraping the Services without Reddit's prior written consent is prohibited"
- **Self-Promotion Rule (90/10)**: "For every 1 time you post self-promotional content, 9 other posts (submissions or comments) should not contain self-promotional content" — applies to ENTIRE account history
- **Reddit Rules**: No spam; no impersonation; no manipulation

### What Other Bots Are Excelling At
- **Claude Routines** for Reddit monitoring and commenting (r/automation, r/MarketingAutomation)
- AI agents that "join conversations" rather than start them
- Pattern: Monitor → Draft response → Human review → Manual post (human-in-the-loop)
- Tools like ReplyAgent.ai, Indexly.ai provide Reddit marketing automation but work around the edges of the rules

### Compliance Assessment for Pat/Maggie Strategy

| Playbook Element | Compliant? | Issue | Required Amendment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Automated DM sending | ❌ **NO** | "No spam through private messages" | DMs must be manually sent, never automated |
| Two accounts (Pat + Maggie) for same entity | ⚠️ **RISKY** | Could be seen as ban evasion/circumvention | Use ONE account only; Maggie is an AI assistant disclosed in bio |
| 90/10 ratio with 15+ comments before promo | ✅ **YES** | Matches Reddit guidance exactly | Keep this requirement; enforce strictly |
| Disclosing AI status in DMs | ✅ **YES** | Reddit requires transparency | Continue this practice |
| Name shared context within 5 words | ✅ **YES** | Personal, contextual outreach | Continue |
| Responding only to public asks | ✅ **YES** | Not cold outreach | Continue |
| Posting exact same comment template | ❌ **NO** | "Posting unoriginal/templated content" | Templates must be heavily personalized; never copy-paste |
| Scraping Reddit for posts to respond to | ❌ **NO** | "Scraping without prior written consent" | Use Reddit's own search manually; do not scrape |
| Using "Pat" persona who isn't real | ⚠️ **RISKY** | Impersonation/misrepresentation if Pat is fictional | Pat must be a real person, or account must clearly state it's run by Assistedly.ai |

### Reddit-Specific Amendments Required
1. **PAT MUST BE REAL**: Reddit's impersonation rules prohibit fictional personas. If "Pat" isn't a real person, the account bio must state "Community manager at Assistedly.ai" not "Pat, a caregiver."
2. **NO AUTOMATED DMs**: The playbook's "Maggie SMS handoff" from Reddit DMs must be manually triggered by a human. Automating DM sending violates ToS.
3. **SCRAPING PROHIBITION**: The playbook cannot recommend scraping Reddit for target posts. Use manual search or Reddit's API (with approval).
4. **TEMPLATE PERSONALIZATION**: Every comment must be 80%+ unique. The playbook's "exact messages" are guidance only — copy-pasting identical comments will trigger spam detection.
5. **ONE ACCOUNT ONLY**: Having Pat (public face) and Maggie (AI assistant) as separate Reddit accounts for the same entity risks being flagged as ban evasion or coordinated inauthentic behavior.

## 2. QUORA — Risk Level: 🟡 MODERATE-HIGH

### Relevant Policies
- **Terms Section 4.4 (Restricted Uses)**: Prohibits "accessing, searching or collecting data from the Quora Platform (through automated or other means, including artificial intelligence or machine learning) to create derivative works... or for other commercial purposes"
- **Terms Section 4.4**: Prohibits using Quora "in any manner that could interfere with, disable, disrupt, overburden, or otherwise impair the Quora Platform"
- **Spam Policy** (extracted from user reports):
  - "Spam is anything irrelevant, fake, or malicious that doesn't add any value"
  - "Promoting products or businesses that aren't relevant is considered spam"
  - "Posting irrelevant or malicious links is considered spam"
  - "Repeatedly posting the same content... without adding value is considered spam"
  - "Fake engagement (such as vote manipulation with multiple accounts) is considered spam"
- **Affiliate Links**: Absolutely prohibited; account-ban risk
- **Acceptable Use Policy**: "No malicious or illegal activity"; "violating the AUP may result in limitation or termination of access"

### What Other Bots Are Excelling At
- **Quora Agent (Claude Routines)**: Runs daily, finds relevant questions, drafts answers — but these typically get banned when detected
- Pattern: AI drafts → Human edits → Manual posting (human-in-the-loop) survives longer
- Many "AI marketing agents" for Quora rely on Q&A-first strategy, not outbound messaging

### Compliance Assessment for Pat/Maggie Strategy

| Playbook Element | Compliant? | Issue | Required Amendment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Automated Quora answer posting | ❌ **NO** | "Automated means to post" prohibited | All answers must be manually posted |
| Including links to Assistedly.ai in answers | ⚠️ **RISKY** | "Promoting products or businesses that aren't relevant" | Links only when DIRECTLY relevant to question; no more than 1 in 10 answers |
| AI-generated answers without disclosure | ❌ **NO** | Quora's AI moderation detects and removes AI content | Must disclose AI assistance; human must heavily edit |
| Two accounts (Pat + Maggie) | ⚠️ **RISKY** | "Fake engagement with multiple accounts" = spam | Use ONE account; disclose AI assistant role |
| Responding to questions with free value first | ✅ **YES** | Core Quora model | Continue |
| DM follow-ups on Quora | ⚠️ **RISKY** | Unsolicited messaging can be spam | Only respond to DMs received; never initiate |
| Scraping Quora for questions | ❌ **NO** | "Accessing/collecting data via automated means" | Use Quora search manually or via approved API |

### Quora-Specific Amendments Required
1. **HEAVY HUMAN EDITING REQUIRED**: AI-drafted answers must be 60%+ rewritten by a human. Quora's AI moderation aggressively removes AI-generated content.
2. **LINKS ARE HIGH-RISK**: The playbook's pattern of ending with "I built a tool..." followed by a link is exactly what Quora flags as spam. Value must be delivered WITHOUT any link in 90% of answers.
3. **NO DM INITIATION**: Quora's messaging should never be used for outbound. Only respond if someone messages Pat first.
4. **DISCLOSE AI IN BIO**: The Pat Richards Quora profile must state "Uses AI research tools" or "Community manager at Assistedly.ai" — not pretend to be an independent caregiver.
5. **NO SCRAPING**: Use Quora's topic feeds and search manually. Do not use Firecrawl or any scraper on Quora.

- **Community Standards — Account Integrity**: "Not misrepresent your identity or affiliation"

- AI tools help draft responses but humans post them
- Pattern: AI monitors group → drafts helpful comment → human reviews → manual post
- Short-form video + polls/quizzes performing well organically
- "Being where conversations happen, not where ads interrupt" is the winning strategy

5. **REFERRAL PROGRAM BOUNDARIES**: The "Share the Safety" program must NEVER be mentioned in public group posts. Only in 1:1 Messenger conversations after the user has already received value.
6. **DISCLOSE AI IN FIRST MESSAGE**: When Maggie (AI) takes over in Messenger, the first message must say "Hi, I'm Maggie, an AI care assistant working with Pat..."

- **Member Agreement**: Prohibits "use of software, devices, scripts, robots, or any other means or processes (including crawlers, browser plugins, and add-ons or any other technology) to scrape the Services"
- **Member Agreement**: Prohibits "gaining or attempting to gain unauthorized access to any part of the Services, including the Accounts of other Members (such as through the use of bots or other automations)"

  - "Posting spam, like unwanted, unsolicited, and/or repeated actions that negatively affect neighbors"

4. **GENUINE NEIGHBOR CONTRIBUTIONS FIRST**: The playbook's "10+ non-commercial helpful replies" is good but insufficient. Nextdoor requires months of genuine neighborhood engagement (lost dog posts, local recommendations, safety alerts) before any business mention.
5. **NO TEMPLATED COMMENTS**: "Posting unoriginal/templated content with no personalization" is explicitly prohibited. Every comment must be neighborhood-specific.
6. **LEAD WITH PERSONAL STORY**: Nextdoor allows business mentions IF framed as "I went through this with my mother in [specific MA town] and here's what I learned..." — never as "Use my service."

## Cross-Platform Bot Activity Insights

### What AI Bots Are Excelling At (Across All Platforms)
Based on current market research, the bots/agencies succeeding in organic outreach follow this pattern:

1. **Claude Routines + Human-in-the-Loop**
   - AI monitors platforms for relevant conversations
   - AI drafts personalized responses
   - **Human reviews and manually posts** every response
   - This avoids "automated posting" violations while scaling output

2. **ReplyAgent.ai Model**
   - Uses AI to find relevant Reddit/Quora threads
   - Generates contextual comment drafts
   - Human edits for tone, accuracy, compliance
   - Posts manually from real account
   - Claims 25-40% reply rates on Reddit when done correctly

3. **Long Game AI**
   - AI tracks conversation history
   - Builds relationship over weeks/months
   - Never pitches in first 3-5 interactions
   - Gradual trust-building before any tool mention

4. **Platform-Native AI (Official)**
   - Reddit: No official brand AI bots permitted
   - Quora: "Quora AI bots" exist but are official platform features
   - Facebook: Meta AI handles responses; third-party AI in DMs is restricted
   - Nextdoor: No third-party AI integration permitted

### The Winning Pattern: AI-Augmented Human, Not AI-Only
Every successful implementation uses AI to **augment** a human community manager, not replace them. The human:
- Owns the account credentials
- Reviews every AI-drafted response
- Personalizes before posting
- Handles all DMs and sensitive conversations
- Takes full responsibility for compliance

- **Action**: Eliminate separate "Pat" and "Maggie" accounts

- **Action**: If Pat is fictional, playbook must shift to brand accounts or hire a real community manager

### Change 5: No Automated DMs Anywhere
- **Action**: All DMs/SMS handoffs must be manually triggered by a human

- **Action**: Playbook "exact messages" become "message frameworks" requiring significant customization
- **Rationale**: All platforms flag copy-pasted templated content as spam

### Change 7: No Scraping on Any Platform
- **Action**: Remove all Firecrawl-based thread identification from ongoing operations

### Change 8: Nextdoor Requires Business Account

### Change 9: Facebook Groups vs. Facebook Page Strategy
- **Action**: Personal profile (real person) for genuine group participation; Business Page for any promotional content
