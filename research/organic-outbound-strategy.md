# Organic Outbound Onboarding Strategy for Assistedly.ai
## Boardy.ai Platform Analysis & Audience-Specific Tactics
### Target: Women in Their 50s (Sandwich Generation) Navigating Assisted Living in Massachusetts

---

## Executive Summary

This document analyzes Boardy.ai’s voice-first AI superconnector model and maps proven organic outbound onboarding tactics from high-growth platforms into Assistedly.ai’s existing strategy. The core insight: **the highest-converting user acquisition for complex, emotional decisions (like finding assisted living) is not passive discovery—it is proactive, trust-building outreach that meets people in their moment of need.**

Boardy.ai proves that an AI agent with a defined personality, proactive voice outreach, and a value-first introduction model can acquire 204,000+ users and facilitate $74B+ in capital introductions with zero traditional advertising. Healthcare AI platforms prove that proactive text/phone outreach at critical care moments drives 2–3× higher engagement than inbound alone. Superhuman proves that mandatory 1:1 onboarding calls create premium brand perception.

For Assistedly.ai, the application is not LinkedIn voice calls. It is **proactive outbound engagement via the channels these women already trust:** Facebook caregiving communities, text message, personalized video, and partnerships with Massachusetts state caregiver programs.

## 1. Platform Analysis: Boardy.ai & Comparable Models

### 1.1 Boardy.ai — The AI Superconnector

**What It Is:**
Boardy.ai is a voice-powered AI agent that proactively calls users after they connect via LinkedIn. Through a conversational interview, it learns the user’s needs, then makes targeted warm introductions to investors, founders, or partners in its network.

**Key Metrics:**
- 204,641+ people spoken with
- 167,997+ introductions made
- $74B+ capital introduced
- Average match time: 17 hours
- Free tier: 3 introductions/day

**Critical Tactics for Assistedly.ai:**

| Boardy Tactic | How It Works | Adaptation for Assistedly.ai |
|---|---|---|
| **Proactive Voice Outreach** | AI calls users after a LinkedIn connection | Massachusetts-licensed “Care Navigator” calls/texts women who download the Revenue Share Calculator but don’t complete it |
| **Personality-Driven Trust** | Australian accent, friendly tone, “old friend” rapport | Create a warm, human-like AI or concierge persona named “Maggie” (common MA name) or “Pat” (trusted caregiver archetype) |
| **Value-First Before Ask** | Makes intros first, asks nothing in return | Send a *free* personalized facility shortlist or cost breakdown before asking for contact info |
| **17-Hour Match Cycle** | Near-instantaneous fulfillment | Promise “Your personalized results in under 2 hours” via AI + human hybrid |
| **Network Effect Onboarding** | Users bring other users to expand the network | “Refer 2 caregiving friends, unlock priority matching + revenue-share boost” |
| **AI-Directed Scarcity** | Free tier capped at 3 intros/day | Free tier: 3 facility comparisons/day. Paid/unlock: unlimited access + human advisor |

**Why Boardy.ai Matters for Assistedly.ai:**
Boardy demonstrates that people will engage with an AI agent for high-stakes personal decisions (investing millions) IF the agent feels trustworthy, personal, and immediately useful. The same principle applies to finding care for a parent—an even more emotionally urgent decision.

### 1.2 Superhuman — Mandatory 1:1 Onboarding

**What It Is:**
Superhuman (email client) required every user to complete a 30-minute 1:1 onboarding call before using the product. This created perceived exclusivity and ensured users understood the value.

**Critical Tactics for Assistedly.ai:**
- **“Care Consultation” Waitlist:** Position signup with language like: *“Due to high demand, each family receives a complimentary 15-minute Care Navigator call to understand your needs before we begin matching.”* This frames the onboarding call as a premium benefit, not a barrier.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Trust Signal:** For women in their 50s researching senior care, a real human voice on the phone before data submission builds the trust necessary to overcome “broker fear.”

### 1.3 Clubhouse — Invite-Only Viral Loops

**What It Is:**
Clubhouse grew to 2M+ users through invite-only access. Each new user received invites to share, creating FOMO and social-proof-driven organic growth.

**Critical Tactics for Assistedly.ai:**
- **“Share the Safety, Not the Secret” Referral Program:** Give every user who completes a match 3 invites to send to caregiving friends. This directly leverages the reality that caregiving is social—women tell other women.
- **Massachusetts-Specific Invite Drops:** Partner with local caregiver advocates to distribute small batches of “early access” invites at Massachusetts caregiver support events.

### 1.4 Healthcare AI Outreach Platforms (Assort Health, Orbita, Hyro)

**What They Do:**
These platforms proactively reach out to patients via text, email, and phone at critical moments in their care journey (e.g., appointment reminders, care gap closures, wellness check-ins).

**Critical Tactics for Assistedly.ai:**
- **Proactive Care Journey SMS:** Trigger outbound text messages at caregiver stress moments. Example: *“Hi [Name], we noticed you downloaded our guide on senior housing costs in Massachusetts. This is a heavy decision—you don’t have to figure it out alone. Reply MATCH for a free personalized facility list, no calls required.”*
- **Signal-Based Outreach:** Use intent signals (clicked calculator, visited pricing page, downloaded guide) to trigger AI-powered but human-supervised outreach within 2 hours.
- **Two-Way Conversation:** Allow users to text back and forth with the “Care Navigator” AI, not just receive broadcast messages.

### 1.5 Massachusetts State & Non-Profit Outreach Channels

**What Exists:**
- Massachusetts Family Caregiver Support Program (free, state-run)
- Aging Services Network of Massachusetts
- Massachusetts Healthy Aging Collaborative
- Local Facebook Groups: “Sandwich Generation Massachusetts,” “Caring for Elderly Parents in MA,” etc.

**Critical Tactic:**
- **Official Referral Partner Status:** Become a listed resource on Mass.gov caregiver pages and within the Family Caregiver Support Program’s referral network. This positions Assistedly.ai as a **state-endorsed alternative** to private brokers—massive trust signal.

## 3. Organic Outbound Onboarding Tactics Mapped to the 7-Part Strategy

### 3.1 Brand Positioning & Messaging (Outbound-Specific Additions)

**Outbound Opening Message for SMS/Email/Community Posts:**
> *“Hi [Name], I’m Pat from Assistedly.ai. I know how overwhelming it is when you’re trying to find safe, affordable housing for your parent—and every site you visit seems to want your phone number so they can sell it. We don’t do that. I help Massachusetts families compare facilities privately, and if you ultimately choose a home through us, we actually share the finder’s fee back with you. Want me to send you a free, no-strings facility list for your area? Reply YES or click here.”*

**Why This Works:**
- **Names a relatable persona** (“Pat”)—not a faceless brand
- **Leads with shared pain** (“overwhelming,” “sell your phone number”)
- **Massachusetts specificity** (local trust signal)
- **Revenue share hook** without requiring a website visit first
- **Zero-friction CTA** (Reply YES)

### 3.2 SEO Traffic Capture + Outbound Signal-Based Engagement

**Tactic: “Search-Plus-Reach” Model**

Traditional SEO captures traffic after the search happens. The outbound layer captures the user *before* or *during* their search journey.

**Implementation:**
1. **Content Syndication in Facebook Groups:** Post the Revenue Share Calculator and comparison guides in Massachusetts caregiver Facebook groups—not as ads, but as genuinely helpful resources. The post should read like a peer recommendation, not a brand promotion.
2. **Intent Signal Monitoring:** Use tools or manually monitor Facebook groups and Reddit (r/agingparents, r/assistedliving) for posts like: *“Does anyone know how to find assisted living in Massachusetts without getting inundated with sales calls?”* Respond authentically with a link to the guide.
3. **Retargeting + SMS Bridge:** When a user visits the site but doesn’t convert, trigger a personalized text (via AI or human) within 2 hours: *“You were looking at assisted living options in [City]. I can send 3 vetted local facilities with real pricing in the next 10 minutes. Just reply with your zip code.”*

### 3.3 Content & On-Page Optimization (Community-Driven Content)

**Tactic: “Caregiver Spotlight” Series**

Instead of generic blog posts, publish anonymized stories of Massachusetts caregivers who used Assistedly.ai:
- *“How Sarah from Worcester avoided 47 sales calls and found her mom’s home in 3 weeks”*
- *“The $8,200 check one Springfield family received after using a revenue-share platform”*

**Outbound Distribution:**
- Share each story in 3–5 Massachusetts Facebook caregiving groups with a genuine caption (not copy-paste)
- Send via email to users who downloaded the calculator but haven’t returned
- Text a link to the most relevant story based on the user’s location

### 3.4 Conversion & Lead Generation Tactics (The AI Concierge Model)

**Tactic A: The “Maggie” AI Concierge**

Build an AI persona (text-first, not voice) named Maggie who lives in SMS and web chat. Maggie:
- Greets users who land on the site: *“Hi, I’m Maggie. I’m here to help you find unbiased options. No one will call you unless you ask. What city is your parent in?”*
- For abandoned calculator sessions: texts within 1 hour with a personalized nudge
- Answers questions about facilities, costs, and the revenue-share model via text
- Knows when to escalate to a human “Senior Care Advisor”

**Tactic B: Proactive SMS Outreach at Caregiver Breakpoints**

Use publicly available data triggers (not invasive scraping) to identify high-stress windows:
- Hospital discharge seasonally peaks (winter falls, January admissions)
- Tax season when families review finances (March–April)
- After a user reads a comparison guide on broker fees

Template: *“Hi [Name], finding assisted living in a hurry after a hospital stay is one of the hardest things families face. I help Massachusetts families do this privately and quickly—no sales calls. Want a 5-minute call? Reply CALL or text STOP.”*

**Tactic C: The “Friend Referral” Onboarding Flow**

- When a user completes a match, present: *“33% of Massachusetts caregivers find homes through a friend’s recommendation. Share your personalized link with 2 caregiving friends and unlock priority matching + a $50 Amazon gift card when they complete a search.”*
- Track referrals in the dashboard. This is the Clubhouse invite model adapted for caregiving communities.

## 7. Risk Mitigation & Trust Safeguards

Because Assistedly.ai differentiates on “no data selling,” outbound tactics must be executed with extreme discipline:

1. **Never purchase lists.** All outreach must be opt-in (calculator users, event attendees, Facebook group members who engaged).
2. **Always include STOP/unsubscribe.** Every SMS and email.
3. **Lead with value, never fear.** No messaging like *“Your parent’s care is urgent”*—that is broker manipulation. Instead: *“This is hard. We can help.”*
4. **No cold calling.** Only scheduled calls after the user has engaged with content.
5. **Transparency badge in every outbound message:** *“Rated by Massachusetts Caregiver Coalition”* (once secured).
6. **AI disclosure:** When Maggie is AI, the user should know: *“I’m Maggie, an AI care assistant. My human colleague Pat reviews every recommendation.”*
