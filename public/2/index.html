How Assistedly Works

Three steps from confusion to clarity.

1

Tell us what matters

Answer a few questions about care needs, location, and budget. Our AI understands nuance — from diabetes management to memory care requirements — so we surface truly relevant options.

  • Natural-language questionnaire
  • Budget and insurance preferences
  • Medical and lifestyle needs
2

Get unbiased matches

We rank facilities using proprietary data — inspections, staff ratios, complaint histories, and resident sentiment — never kickbacks or placement fees. What you see is what families actually experience.

  • No paid placements or sponsored listings
  • Real-time state inspection data
  • Resident & family sentiment analysis

Book tours with confidence

Compare side-by-side, read curated inspection highlights, and schedule visits directly. We even generate a personalized question checklist for every tour so you never miss the details that matter.

  • Side-by-side comparison tool
  • Custom tour question checklists
  • Cost & contract transparency
